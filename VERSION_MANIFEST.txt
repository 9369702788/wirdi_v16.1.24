WIRDI v252 -- Notification Diagnostics & Repair screen
================================================================================
Consolidates 3 suggestions from the latest review into ONE screen:
- Live status: notification permission, exact-alarm permission, and a
  GROUND-TRUTH count of what's actually scheduled with Android right now
  (via the plugin's own pendingNotificationRequests() -- not just what
  this app's SharedPreferences bookkeeping THINKS is scheduled).
- One-tap "Repair Notifications" button: re-requests permissions, re-fetches
  prayer times, reschedules everything (reusing the exact same code path
  as normal operation, no separate logic), then re-verifies against
  Android and reports exactly what happened in plain language.
- The existing test buttons (immediate + scheduled) are kept, now grouped
  together in the same screen instead of scattered lower down.

NOT implemented from the same review (explicit decision, explained to
Moustafa in chat): full SharedPreferences architecture refactor, removing
every catch (_) {} across the whole codebase, and a full automated test
suite -- all valuable long-term but large, separate undertakings not
suited to bundle into this focused patch.

Files:
1) lib/core/services/notification_service.dart (adds pendingRequests(); includes all earlier v242/v249/v251 fixes)
2) lib/features/settings/notification_diagnostics_screen.dart (NEW)
3) lib/features/settings/settings_screen.dart (adds navigation entry point; includes all earlier fixes)
